# Privacy and safety

## Your data

Aura collects only what it needs to match you: the preferences you give at
onboarding and your email for sign-in. The app does not ask for a phone number
to create your profile.

## How AI uses your input

Ora turns your description into structured preferences and a numeric profile used
for matching. Your raw words are not shared with other users; other people see
only the plan and the shared ground, never your full input.

## Numbers stay private

When Ora connects a group, it does so without exposing personal phone numbers.
The introduction happens through Aura, so you meet people without handing over
your contact details up front.

## Match explanations are suggestions

The reasons Ora gives for a match are suggestions about why a group could get
along, based on shared signals. They are not certainties, and Ora does not claim
to know you better than you do, at least not yet.

## You stay in control

You can refine or correct what Ora captured, decline any plan, and withdraw from
a plan you can no longer make.
