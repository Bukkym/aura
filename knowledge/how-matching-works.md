# How matching works

## Deterministic, explainable matching

Aura's matching is deterministic, not a black box. Ora compares your structured
preferences against other people in your city using a weighted overlap score, so
every match can be explained in plain language. The score is interpretable and
feeds the "why this plan" copy you see on a plan card.

## What the score weighs

The match score weighs several signals. Personality fit and shared interests
count the most, followed by the activities you like, your vibe, your social
preferences, the kind of connection you are after, and your neighborhoods. The
goal is a group where the people genuinely fit, not just anyone nearby.

## Archetypes

Every person is grouped into one of seven archetypes, a compatibility pool of
people who tend to fit together. Your archetype comes from your onboarding
answers. Matches are drawn from your pool so a new person lands among people who
overlap with them.

## Why these people

When Ora shows a plan, it tells you the shared ground it found: the interests,
vibe, or activities you have in common with the group. These explanations are
suggestions about why the group could click, not guarantees.

## Refining a match

If a plan is not quite right, you can tell Ora in plain language: more outdoorsy,
a smaller group, a chill crowd, a different night. Ora re-matches on what you
said. These corrections are how Ora gets better at reading you over time.
