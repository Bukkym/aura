# Plans

## What a plan is

A plan is an introduction Ora builds for you: one activity, one venue with a real
address, a date and time, and a few participants. A plan card shows the activity,
the place, the vibe, and Ora's reason for the match.

## Plan states

A plan is "ready" until you act on it. You can accept it, which confirms it, or
decline it, which dismisses it. Declined plans are kept on record but filtered
out of your active list.

## Requesting and withdrawing

You can ask Ora for a new plan at any time. You can also withdraw from your
current plan if you can no longer make it; under the pool model, withdrawing your
own request is how you leave. When you withdraw, your seat frees up.

## The venue

Plans use real Berlin venues with street addresses, chosen to fit the group's
activity and vibe. The venue is picked to match the shared ground, for example a
cozy cafe for a laid-back group or a boulder gym for an active one.

## Privacy in a plan

You meet people through the plan, not through exposed contact details. Numbers
stay private; introductions happen inside Aura.
