# About Aura

## What Aura is

Aura is a social app that introduces you to a small group of compatible people
and a place to meet them in your city. No swiping and no public profiles. You
tell Ora what you are like and who you want to meet, and Ora builds a plan: an
activity, a venue, and a few people Ora thinks you will get along with.

## Who Ora is

Ora is the AI inside Aura. Ora listens to what you say, turns it into structured
preferences, finds your people, explains why it picked them, and refines the
plan when you push back. Over time Ora learns from what you actually do, not just
what you said.

## The core loop

You make a request (what you want to do, when you are free, who you want to
meet). The system turns requests into plans. You can accept a plan, decline it,
or ask Ora to change it. Everyone in a plan is a participant who made their own
request, so a plan is a group of people who each opted in.

## What makes Aura different

Most apps match you on what you self-report and take it at face value. Aura is
built to close the gap between what people say they want and what their behavior
shows they actually enjoy. The match explanation, the reason Ora gives for each
plan, is the heart of the product.
