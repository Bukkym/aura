# Getting started

## Onboarding

When you join, Ora asks you a few quick things: who you are (personality and
vibe), what pulls your attention (interests and activities), where you are and
when you are free, and who you want to meet. You can talk to Ora or tap through
chips. From your answers, Ora builds your first plan.

## Talking to Ora

You can describe yourself in your own words and Ora extracts the preferences it
needs. If something important is missing, Ora asks a follow-up question until it
has enough to make a good first match. You can always edit what Ora captured.

## What Ora needs for a good match

To match you well, Ora needs a sense of your personality, your interests and the
activities you like, your neighborhoods and availability, and the kind of
connection you are after. The more honest the input, the better the plan.

## After your first plan

Once you have a plan you can accept it, ask for changes, or request another.
Aura is about repeated low-pressure meetings, so the value grows as you keep
showing up and Ora learns what actually works for you.
